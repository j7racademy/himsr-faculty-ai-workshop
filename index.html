THE STARTING POINT

A useful tool begins
with a better question.

AI can help us draft, organize, and explore. Every medical or academic claim still needs a person who knows the context and checks the evidence.

01

Give it a role

Name the educator or research perspective you want it to use.

02

Bound the source

Say which document or data it may use, and what it should do when information is missing.

03

Ask for a checkable output

Request a format, citations, uncertainties, and review points.

5 MIN FRAME15 MIN DEMO + TRY5 MIN DISCUSS
THE PROMPT / COPY & USE

Prepare an exploratory literature scoping brief (2022–2026) on cost-effective biomarkers and AI-assisted risk stratification for early diabetic nephropathy in South Asian populations. Prioritize peer-reviewed sources, PubMed/PMC, and relevant Indian guidance. Summarize study designs, populations and sample sizes, reported diagnostic metrics only when explicit, limitations, and three research gaps. Provide working links for each factual claim, distinguish evidence from inference, and flag missing or conflicting evidence. Do not call this a systematic review.

Use a completed report so the room never waits for generation. A scoping brief is a starting point; a formal review needs a documented search strategy, eligibility criteria, and independent appraisal.

0615:05–15:30
THE HUMAN CHECK

Useful AI. Clear
responsibility.

Before an output reaches a classroom, chart, protocol, or paper, the responsible faculty member checks it and follows institutional policy.

01 / PRIVACY

Protect patient information

Use fictional, public, or institutionally approved material. Do not enter identifiable HAHC patient details into public AI tools.

02 / EVIDENCE

Verify sources and claims

Open citations, check clinical details, and flag what the source cannot support.

03 / CONTEXT

Check local relevance

Review Indian guidance, populations, and the institution’s own procedures before use.

04 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Own the final work

Disclose AI assistance where required and keep the clinician, educator, or investigator responsible for the final decision.

DISCUSS · 5 MIN

A resident has a ward summary and wants a teaching case.

What should happen before any text is pasted into an AI tool?

Discuss: use a fictional or approved, fully de-identified case; remove direct and indirect identifiers; confirm institutional permissions and tool policy; review every generated fact before teaching.
